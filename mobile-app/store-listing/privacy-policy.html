Privacy Policy

Crooked Guess (Android app com.soham.crookedguess and website) · Effective 23 September 2026

In short: Crooked Guess has no accounts and doesn't send your scores or settings anywhere; they stay on your device. The Android app uses Google AdMob to show an optional video when you ask for an extra hint, and Google collects some device data to do that (details below). The LAN Versus mode talks only to the other player's device on your own Wi-Fi or hotspot.

Who we are

Crooked Guess is made by Soham, an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email soham1studio@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves these on your device only (in the app's local storage), so it can remember them between sessions:

  • your settings (level, Time Attack, sound on/off, tutorial finished);
  • the player name you type (optional);
  • your scoreboard: high scores, statistics, streaks, recent games and your Versus record against the names of people you played.

We never receive this information. You can erase it at any time with Scoreboard > Reset scoreboard, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

LAN Versus (playing a friend nearby)

When you use Versus, your device connects directly to the other player's device over your local network (the same Wi-Fi, or a phone's hotspot). What is exchanged: the player names you each typed, the chosen level, the shared secret code for the round, how many guesses each player has made, how many pegs were right in each guess, and who finished first. While a phone is hosting, it announces the game (host name and level) on the local network so nearby players can find it. This traffic stays on your local network. It is not sent to us or to any server on the internet. It is also not encrypted, so only play Versus on networks you trust.

Ads (Android app only)

The Android app shows a rewarded video only when you choose to watch one to earn an extra hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address (used to estimate approximate location)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (e.g. an ad was shown or tapped)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (e.g. app launch time, hangs)Ad performance and analytics

This data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change it later in the app: How to play (?) > Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Privacy > Ads or Google > Ads).

The website version

The website shows no ads and has no tracking. It loads its font from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when the page loads (Google Fonts privacy FAQ). Scores and settings are stored in your browser only.

Children

Crooked Guess is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, email us and we will delete it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, contacts, camera, microphone or location permission.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't have a server that stores it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
