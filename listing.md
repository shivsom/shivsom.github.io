## Full description (paste as is, about 1,700 of 4,000 characters)

A secret code of coloured pegs is hidden. Crack it before you run out of guesses.

Sounds like the classic code-breaker? Here's the crooked part: after every guess you only learn HOW MANY pegs are the right colour in the right place. Never which ones. No "right colour, wrong spot" clues either. Every guess is a small logic puzzle, and cracking the code feels great.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Fill every slot with a colour and press Guess. Colours can repeat.
• The number next to your guess tells you how many pegs are exactly right.
• Stuck? Use a hint to check one peg: ✓ or ✗. Hints never cost a guess.
• Out of hints? Watch a short video for an extra one (optional, up to 3 per game).

THREE LEVELS, ONE CLOCK
• Easy: 4 slots, 4 colours, 12 guesses.
• Medium: 4 slots, 6 colours, 10 guesses.
• Hard: 5 slots, 6 colours, 10 guesses.
• Time Attack: switch on the clock for any level.

VERSUS: RACE A FRIEND
Two phones on the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot) get the same secret code. The first to crack it wins the round. You can see how many guesses your rival has made and how close they are, but never their colours. No account and no internet needed.

SCOREBOARD
Earn points for every win: fewer guesses, unused hints and a fast clock score more. Chase your top 10, streaks and fastest times for every mode, and keep a head-to-head record against your friends.

MADE WITH CARE
• A one-minute guided tutorial with Pip, a friendly peg.
• A fireworks show every time you crack a code.
• Every colour also has a symbol (▲ ● ■ ◆ ★ ✚), so it's playable with colour blindness.
• Sound effects you can switch off with one tap.
• Your scores stay on your phone. No sign-up.

Can you crack the crooked code?
